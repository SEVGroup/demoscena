# Competition check — 28 October 2026

Status: local prototype; NOT universally competition compliant. Rules below belong to the stated editions, most of which have already finished. They do not establish admission to a future edition.

## Technical checks

- Single native arm64 executable; final signed size is enforced by source/build.sh against 65,536 bytes. The ZIP size is not the relevant number.
- Only Apple system frameworks, procedural graphics embedded. No runtime sidecar files, external media, third-party runtimes or internet downloads.
- Full windowed run completed successfully with exit code 0 after its 96-second timeline. Initial shader compilation adds startup time. Final deployment-target-only rebuild was also compiled and signed successfully.
- Esc and Cmd+Q handlers implemented; manual keyboard response still needs confirmation.
- Window and fullscreen supported. External projector, fullscreen switching and sustained framerate on competition hardware remain untested.
- GPU rendering of each of four scenes passed on this M2. Local ad-hoc signature verification passed. Not notarized or tested on a second Mac.
- A quiet filtered white-noise ambient bed plays without percussion.
- Author handle/group, final title/credits and actual target competition remain to be chosen. AI usage must be disclosed.

## Revision 2026

https://2026.revision-party.net/competitions/pc/
https://2026.revision-party.net/competitions/general-rules/

64K executable <=65,536 bytes; duration <=8 minutes including loading/precalc; instant Esc or Alt-F4 exit; ZIP/RAR delivery. Mac is explicitly listed, but exact Mac hardware is unannounced on this page and external-display operation is required. General rules prohibit generative AI content. They do not give an explicit exception for this AI-written entry. Consequently this prototype's admission is NOT established; disclose its provenance to organizers before considering submission. It is incorrect to claim Revision only accepts Windows.

## Evoke 2026

https://2026.evoke.eu/competitions/
https://2026.evoke.eu/competitions/executable/

General rules explicitly prohibit AI usage/content, require tool disclosure, an unreleased entry and attendance by at least one group member. Current AI-authored prototype does not satisfy the stated AI policy. Changing platform or shrinking the binary does not fix that.

## Deadline 2026

https://www.demoparty.berlin/competitions/

PC Intro executable limit: 65,536 bytes. AI policy explicitly requires entry-specific, non-framework code to be written by a human. This prototype fails that requirement. A Wild submission is not an exemption from the general AI rules. Remote executable submissions are otherwise supported; alternative hardware is coordinated with organizers.

## NOVA 2026 — compatible policy reference

https://2026.novaparty.org/compos/

New School Intro accepts <=65,536 bytes. Mac entries accepted with your own hardware. AI/ML is permitted subject to explicit disclosure of how it was used, shown on the beamslide. Remote entries accepted. This is a plausible policy match, not a guarantee of admission or a future slot. For a future edition verify its new rules, arrange Mac playback and provide a high-quality audiovisual recording as fallback.
